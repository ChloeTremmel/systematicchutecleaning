Why clean the full vent path?

The lint you can’t see matters most.

The lint screen catches only part of what each load produces. Material that escapes can settle along the vent line, around turns, and near exterior exits—reducing airflow and increasing fire risk over time.

What are signs that a dryer vent may need cleaning?

Longer drying times, unusually hot laundry or appliances, a burning smell, visible lint near the exterior vent, and weak airflow can all signal restriction. Stop using the dryer and seek professional help if you notice overheating or a burning odor.

Do you clean dryer chutes in apartment buildings?

Yes. The service is designed for both individual vents and shared or extended dryer-chute systems in multi-unit properties, subject to an on-site assessment.

How long does an appointment take?

Timing depends on the vent length, access, number of dryers, and level of buildup. We’ll provide a clearer estimate after learning about your property.

Can you work with property managers and associations?

Yes. We can discuss access, scheduling, unit coordination, and the scope needed for multi-unit or commercial properties.
